# Contributing to POND

Thanks for wanting to grow the POND. You don't need a doctorate, a server farm, or a crypto wallet to take part.

## Join the community
- Say hello on [Discord](https://discord.gg/kNvtcjPmJ7) and introduce yourself and what you build.
- Come to the [PONDcast](https://www.pond.is/p/pondcast-weekly-meeting-for-the-pond), our weekly meeting, Thursdays at 6:30 PM PT on Discord.
- Share projects and ideas on [r/PONDAI](https://www.reddit.com/r/PONDAI/).
- Questions about the Foundation: [foundation@pond.is](mailto:foundation@pond.is).

## Suggest a project for awesome-pond
1. Fork the repo and add one line to the right category in `README.md`:
   `- [Name](https://link) - One-line description ending with a period.`
2. Keep the list alphabetical within its section if you can.
3. Open a pull request saying which POND principles the project meets and how.

You're welcome to add your own project.

## The inclusion bar: the POND Principle
A project should be **open source** and clearly advance at least one of these, without working against the others:
- **Personal & Private**: the user's data, memory and context serve the user, under boundaries they understand and control.
- **On-Device & On-Premise**: it runs, or can run, on hardware the user owns.
- **Nodal & Networked**: personal doesn't mean isolated. It can connect to others and share only what the user chooses.
- **Decentralized & Distributed**: no single company has to sit in the middle.

We'll usually decline projects that require a mandatory cloud account, collect telemetry without consent, are closed source, or are mainly about tokens or speculation. We value working systems over slogans.

## Other contributions
Fixes to descriptions, dead-link reports and new categories are welcome as issues or PRs. Please be constructive and kind; disagreement is fine, conformity isn't required.
